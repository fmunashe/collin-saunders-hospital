1Executive Summary

The Hospital Management System (HMS) is a web-based platform that digitises the core clinical and administrative operations of Collin Saunders Hospital. Phase 1 delivers three tightly integrated modules — Outpatient, Inpatient, and Pharmacy — supported by billing, referrals, role-based access control, and a comprehensive reporting suite.

The system is built on Laravel with Laravel Nova as the administrative interface, providing a secure, auditable, and maintainable foundation that can scale into laboratory, theatre, and radiology modules in Phase 2.

Design principle: Automate what should be automatic. Bed occupancy, stock deduction, invoice totals, and prescription statuses are all driven by the system — reducing manual error and keeping data trustworthy.

5Functional Modules

5.1 Outpatient

  • Patient registration with auto-generated sequential numbers (PT00001).
  • Inpatient / Outpatient status badge derived from active admissions.
  • Visit lifecycle: waiting → in progress → completed.
  • One-click "Generate Invoice" from a visit (consultation fee + dispensed medications).

5.2 Inpatient

  • Admissions linked to ward, bed, doctor, and department.
  • Automatic bed occupancy — a bed becomes occupied on admission and is freed on discharge, transfer, or death.
  • Double-booking prevention — a bed cannot host two admitted patients.
  • Inpatient medication administration with route, dosage, and stock deduction.

5.3 Pharmacy

  • Medication catalogue with stock quantity, reorder level, unit price, expiry date.
  • Prescriptions with line items; single and bulk dispensing.
  • Guards: cannot dispense beyond available stock or expired medication.
  • Automatic prescription status sync: pending → partially dispensed → dispensed.
  • Complete stock movement ledger (received, dispensed, returned, adjustment, expired).

8Workflows

8.1 Outpatient Visit → Invoice

Receptionist registers patient → books Visit (waiting) ↓ Doctor consults, records diagnosis → Visit (completed) ↓ Doctor writes Prescription → Pharmacy dispenses (stock deducted) ↓ Billing runs "Generate Invoice" → Invoice (pending) ↓ Payment captured → Invoice (paid / partially paid) → PDF issued

8.2 Inpatient Admission → Discharge

Doctor admits patient, assigns available Bed ↓ (bed auto-marked OCCUPIED, double-booking blocked) Nurse administers medication (stock deducted, audit logged) ↓ Doctor discharges patient ↓ (bed auto-marked AVAILABLE, discharge time stamped) Admission invoice finalised

10Security & Access Control

  • Authentication: Laravel Fortify with optional two-factor authentication and passkeys.
  • Authorisation: Spatie permission model — every resource has a policy checking granular permissions (resource-action).
  • Report gating: each report requires a dedicated view-*-reports permission.
  • Audit trail: all model changes recorded as Nova Action Events, exposed as a read-only Audit Log resource.
  • Data protection: UUID keys, hashed passwords, encrypted sessions, PII handled per POPIA principles.

  • End-user devices and peripherals (printers, scanners) are procured by the client.
