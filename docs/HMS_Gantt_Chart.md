## Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|--------|------------|
| Requirements creep | High | Medium | Strict change control, 2-week sprint boundaries |
| Client availability | Medium | High | Schedule workshops in advance, async communication |
| Travel disruptions | Low | Medium | Remote alternatives for non-critical visits |
| Technical complexity (integrations) | Medium | Medium | Spike research in Sprint 1, early POCs |
| Staff turnover | Low | High | Documentation, knowledge sharing, pair programming |
